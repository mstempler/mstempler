Evidence of implementation is not proof of individual mastery or production readiness.

## Interpretation
Levels describe artifact evidence, not proficiency ratings. AI-assisted commits do not establish the owner’s individual design contribution. Confirm personal responsibilities before interviews or publication. CI is not CD; cloud deployment is not established by container configuration.
