# Security and disclosure

This portfolio intentionally excludes proprietary source, credentials, customer records, internal identifiers, exact production topology and sensitive business rules.

Before publication, review contractual rights, third-party attribution, secret exposure and metadata. Do not submit sensitive material through public issues. Contact the owner privately through an agreed channel.

No security certification is implied. Publication requires explicit owner approval.

## Release gate
The owner must confirm rights, contractual restrictions and permitted attribution; review all files and metadata for secrets and sensitive identifiers; and approve the exact release. A clean portfolio scan does not establish that source repositories or their histories are clean. Public copies cannot reliably be recalled. No private repository links or customer data should be added.
